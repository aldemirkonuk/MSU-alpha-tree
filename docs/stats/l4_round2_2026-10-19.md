## What it shows

- **Torvik reproduced exactly.** Barthag uses exponent 11.5 for 2025-26 (solved per team: every team gives 11.5). Torvik's possessions are the two-team averaged estimate with c_FT = 0.475, our registry default: MSU's 20 conference games sum to Torvik's conference possessions within 0.24 of about 1,323 (0.02%). Conference points, records and win % match exactly, and ConOE = ConPF / ConPoss holds for all teams.
- **KenPom identities hold** for all 365 teams within display rounding: AdjEM = AdjOE − AdjDE and SOS AdjEM = SOS AdjOE − SOS AdjDE. KenPom's adjustment itself stays reference only (proprietary).
- **Play-by-play reproduces the box:** largest lead 68/68, first-half points 67/68, and every MSU player's points and field-goal attempts 386/386.
- **Where sources disagree by definition:**
  - CBBD tags the Big Ten Tournament game as a regular-season conference game; Torvik does not. Filter conference stats by game type.
  - CBBD's 'rim' shot range is not the paint (points in the paint proxy within 4 points on 38% of team-games); points in the paint need shot coordinates.
  - The official fast-break points follow the scorer's judgement, not a clock rule: a 7 s window after a change of possession lands within 4 points on 59% of team-games (that gap is metric M-TRN-015).
  - CBBD's Elo is not reproduced by our Elo (best win/loss Elo K = 38, H = 50: 4/70 updates within 2) nor by a linear margin model (9/70). It is stored as an external, undocumented rating.
